      Run a test

      A guided script — eyes open, eyes closed, talking, yawning — spoken and shown on the video. What the app sees is compared with what the tester was told to do. Starts with a 3-second calibration. Requirement: ≥ 90 %.

      Each trial: hide the object, then show it at the tone and hold it. The recorded frames are checked afterwards to find when it really appeared, so reaction time doesn't count. Requirement: ≤ 1 s.

      While the test runs there are no alerts and the vehicle stays stopped. Results are saved in this session.
